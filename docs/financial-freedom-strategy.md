# Financial Freedom Strategy

## Purpose

Define the system for increasing income, building assets, controlling risk, and improving financial independence over time.

## Current Financial Objective

- **Primary target:** Generate $1,000/month in scalable, non-time-bounded income from tech products within 12 months.
- **Target date or horizon:** May 2027
- **Why this target matters:** $1,000/month in Indonesia provides significant lifestyle optionality. More importantly, it proves the model works and can be scaled.

## Strategy Pillars

## Income Engine Map

- **Primary income source:** Salary (current day job) — stable, time-bounded.
- **Secondary income source:** Freelance development — variable, time-bounded.
- **Scalable income opportunities:**
  - Solo-leveling brain as a SaaS template or open-source product with sponsorship.
  - Dashboard widgets or components as a paid UI kit.
  - Wedding-invitation as a white-label digital invite service.
  - Affiliate revenue from recommending tools used in the stack (Firebase, Cloudflare, etc.).
- **Skills that improve earnings power:**
  - AI-native engineering (can deliver 3x faster than traditional dev).
  - Full-stack deployment (no dependency on DevOps teams).
  - Indonesian market product design (underserved by global tools).

## Asset Plan

- **Cash reserve policy:** Maintain 6 months of expenses in liquid savings. Never drop below 3 months.
- **Investment approach:**
  - Begin DCA (dollar-cost averaging) into global index funds via Indonesian broker (e.g., Bibit, Stockbit).
  - Allocate 20% of any product revenue to reinvestment in growth.
- **Business or project assets to build:**
  - Content library: blog posts, tutorials, case studies about building with AI.
  - Open-source reputation: well-documented repos that attract contributors and sponsors.
  - Email list: audience of developers and solopreneurs interested in AI-native workflows.
- **Debt reduction or avoidance rules:**
  - No consumer debt for non-essential purchases.
  - If financing is needed for growth, cap at 10% of monthly income.

## Guardrails

- **Maximum acceptable downside:** Losing 3 months of runway on any single experiment. If an experiment burns through that, stop and reassess.
- **Spending rules:**
  - Infrastructure/tools: unlimited if they save time or enable new capabilities.
  - Learning: unlimited if it directly applies to active projects.
  - Lifestyle: increase only when product revenue exceeds current salary.
- **Decision thresholds for major purchases:**
  - < $50: decide immediately.
  - $50–$200: 24-hour wait.
  - > $200: requires written justification in this doc.
- **Rules for new commitments or risks:**
  - No new major project until current active priority is marked DONE in `AI_CONTEXT.md`.
  - Any new income stream must have a 4-week MVP plan before starting.
