## Thought Process

- Human PM best practices → AI team patterns
- AIs don't remember → coordination via persistent docs/git
- AIs work async → handoff notes instead of real-time sync
- Specialization → matching task to AI expertise
- Scaling → increasing structure as team grows

## Conclusion

Multi-AI teamwork requires explicit structure: roles, ownership, async handoff protocol, persistent state. PM AI is the glue. Treat agents as team members with specializations.
