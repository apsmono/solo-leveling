## Question / Answer Log

### Q: What are the core roles?

Strategic Planner, Executor, Coordinator, Reviewer, Domain Expert

### Q: How to avoid conflicts?

Task ownership + handoff summaries + git-based state

### Q: What does PM AI do?

Breaks work, assigns, tracks, facilitates, resolves, maintains quality gates

### Q: How to scale?

Document-based coordination, explicit handoff protocol, task queue, role hierarchy

### Q: When do you need a PM?

At 3+ AIs, you need coordination; formal PM role at 5+
